What does it cost to lose the ball here?

A pass risk lens for SkillCorner data. Every pass, and every option the passer had, is priced in expected goals conceded: the chance it fails (SkillCorner's pass model) times what losing the ball where it would die costs (learned from open-play turnovers). It shows the trade-off, not a verdict.

1 · The cost map

Expected goals conceded per 100 balls lost at each point (a rival shot within 15 s × goals per shot). Your own goal is on the left. Hover the pitch.

2 · Pass explorer

Pick a match and a team, then a pass. Arrows are the options the passer had, labelled with their chance of arriving; the solid one is the pass played. Colour = risk on a fixed scale (safe → ≥10), in expected goals conceded per 1,000 such passes.

3 · Team risk profiles

Risk of the passes each team played, per 1,000 passes, and the share of it created in their own third. Teams have 1–7 matches in this sample: indicative, not a ranking.

How it works, and its limits

Cost surface: logistic model of a rival shot within 15 s by where the ball was lost (chosen on held-out matches, calibrated). Where a failed pass dies: replayed from real misplaced passes (most die near the intended receiver). Validation: on matches the model never saw, the risk predicts passes followed by a rival shot better than SkillCorner's failure probability alone. Limits: the cost is immediate danger, not the value of losing possession high up; 20 matches of one league; it does not tell which option was "right" — that was tested and is not supported.
